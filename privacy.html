Privacy Policy

13 November 2026

Foldfight collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The two player names you type, so you don't have to enter them again. They never leave the device and you can change or clear them any time.
  • Tonight's score, which is kept only while the app is open.

Purchases

The one-time “Unlock All Games” purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks StoreKit on your device whether the purchase exists.

The hinge

On iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge angle on the device to run Fuse and Angle Hunt. The angle is used only while you play and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Foldfight does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: playauragames@gmail.com.

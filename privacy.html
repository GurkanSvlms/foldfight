Privacy Policy

Updated 13 November 2026

Foldfight collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The two player names you type stay on your device so you don't have to enter them again. Tonight's score is kept only while the app is open.

Purchases

The one-time “Unlock All Games” purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

The hinge

On iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge angle only while you play Fuse and Angle Hunt. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Foldfight collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Questions: playauragames@gmail.com.

Support

Something not working, or an idea for a new game? Write to playauragames@gmail.com. We usually answer within two days.

Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap any locked game, then “Restore purchase” under the unlock button. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. Every game works on every iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 or later. On iPhone Duo, Fuse and Angle Hunt use the real hinge.

How do two people play on one phone?

Lay the phone flat between you. Each half of the screen belongs to one player and its text faces that player.

Is there a subscription or ads?

No. Fuse is free. One purchase unlocks all games, including the ones we add later.

Can I change the player names?

Yes. Tap “Edit names” on the home screen.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on the phone; only the purchase talks to the App Store.
